Resource Guard

Protects VPS by blocking or cancelling panel-managed heavy work when available RAM cannot safely cover it.

Operation blocking

RAM kept off-limits at all times. Every operation is blocked if available RAM minus this reserve is less than what it needs. Recommended: about 15% of total RAM. Examples: 150 MB on 1 GB VPS, 300 MB on 2 GB VPS, 600 MB on 4 GB VPS.

Visual warnings

When total VPS RAM usage crosses this percentage, a warning banner appears on every page. This does not block operations.
